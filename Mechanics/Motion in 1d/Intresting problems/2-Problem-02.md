### IE Irodov: Physical Fundamentals of Mechanics [Q1.3]

# Problem summary:

 A car starts from rest moves with acceleration of 5m/s^2 for some distance, 
 and then with uniform speed for some distance and finally deccelerates at 
 same rate (5m/s^2). For this journey its avg. speed is 72KmpH and total time
 taken is 25sec. and we have to find the time for which it travelled uniformly.

# Approach:

Use proper manipulations and Equation of Motion.
